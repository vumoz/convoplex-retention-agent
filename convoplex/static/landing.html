Convoplex Retention Agent, built in Lagos for Nigerian retailers.

A shop loses a regular, and the next message never goes out. This agent drafts the win-back on WhatsApp, with the real naira price, and answers in the language the customer asked for.

View live demo GitHub repo

The problem

Nigerian provision shops, fabric stalls, and phone sellers keep their customers on WhatsApp, not on a website. When someone stops buying, the shopkeeper is already on the shop floor. There is no quiet hour to write a personal offer, check the shelf, and follow up in the customer’s own wording. Regulars lapse, and the bag of rice they used to buy goes to another shop.

What the agent does

It scores who has gone quiet, then writes a win-back with a discount taken from the shelf price, in naira. A promo card is drawn for that offer. A voice note is transcribed and answered like a typed reply. The default reply is formal English. A customer can opt in, by typing or by voice, to Broken English (Nigerian Pidgin), Igbo, Yoruba, or Hausa. Prices stay in ₦. Product names stay as they are on the shelf.

Meta models

  • muse-spark-1.3 drafts the win-back and the reply, and calls the shop tools for stock, price, the offer, and the order.
  • muse-image-1.0 draws a text-free product photo. The naira prices are printed on afterwards so the card matches the offer.
  • muse-voice-transcribe-1.0 turns a WhatsApp voice note into text before the same reply path runs.

Try it

The live demo is synthetic data only: fictional shops, customers, orders, and prices. Phone numbers are masked to the last two digits. The demo page cannot send a WhatsApp message or change the book. The admin dashboard stays at /admin.
